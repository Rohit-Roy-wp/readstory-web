Ramesh pichhle teis saal se purani mill ka night watchman tha. Har raat, 10 baje se 6 baje tak, woh khaali corridors me torch aur ek lakdi ki chhadi le kar patrol karta tha.

Kabhi kuch nahi hota tha. Kabhi kuch nahi badalta tha.

Siwaye us aurat ke.

Woh har raat theek 2:47 AM par aati thi — ek minute pehle ya baad nahi. Woh safed saree pehenti, teesri manzil ki tooti hui khidki ke paas khadi hoti, aur nadi ko dekhti rehti.

Ramesh kabhi uske paas nahi gaya. Woh bas door se dekhta rehta, har raat, saalon tak.

Ek raat, usne aakhir himmat juti. Woh seedhiyaan chadha, torch uske haath me kaanp rahi thi.

"Madam?" usne awaaz di. "Kaun ho aap?"

Woh mudi. Uska chehra shaant tha, udaas, jaana-pehchaana.

"Tum mujhe yaad nahi, Ramesh?" usne dheere se kaha. "Tum hi the jisne mujhe nadi se bahar nikala tha. Chalis saal pehle."

Uske ghutne jhuk gaye. Usse yaad aaya — ek jawaan dulhan jo kood gayi thi, ek jawaan aadmi jo usse bacha nahi paya tha.

"Main intezaar kar rahi thi," usne kaha. "Shukriya kehne ke liye. Koshish karne ke liye."

Woh gaayab ho gayi. Aur agle din subah, Ramesh ne istifa de diya. Usne dobara kabhi nadi ko nahi dekha.
