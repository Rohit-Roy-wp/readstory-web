Platform khaali tha — sirf ek awaara kutta aur ek pachaas saal ka aadmi, apne suitcase ke saath. Arjun thandi bench par baitha tha, 12:45 wali train ka intezaar karte hue. Teen din se soya nahi tha.

Train late aayi, jaise aadhi raat ki trains aksar late aati hain. Usne ek khaali compartment dhundha aur baith gaya, andhere ko dekhte hue.

Do station baad, ek buzurg aurat chadhi. Uski aankhen narm thi aur uske paas santre ka ek chhota thaila tha. Bina poochhe, woh uske saamne baith gayi aur ek santra badhaya.

"Thake hue lag rahe ho, beta," usne kaha.

Arjun ka gala bhar aaya. Saalon ho gaye the kisi ne usse itne pyaar se baat ki thi.

Woh ghanton baat karte rahe — uske marhoom pati ke baare me, Arjun ke failed business ke baare me, train ke safar ke us ajeeb se sukoon ke baare me. Jab woh apne station par utri, usne dus rupaye ka sikka Arjun ki hatheli par rakh diya.

"Kismat ke liye," usne kaha. "Aur yaad rakhna, har andhera ek nayi subah ke saath khatam hota hai."

Train chalti rahi. Arjun ne sikke ko dekha, phir aasman ko. Saalon me pehli baar, woh muskuraya.
