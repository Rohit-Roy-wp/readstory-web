The shop had been there for as long as anyone could remember. No sign, no name — just a wooden door and the smell of old paper drifting through the cracks.

Riya discovered it by accident one Sunday afternoon while avoiding the rain. Inside, the shelves reached the ceiling. Thousands of books, all covered in dust.

The owner, an old man with silver hair, didn't look up from his reading. "Take your time," he said. "Books choose their readers."

She wandered for an hour. Then a small leather-bound book caught her eye — it had no title, no author, just a faded sketch of a rose on the cover.

When she opened it, the first page read:

> *"If you found this book, it means you were meant to. Write your story here. Pass it forward."*

The rest of the pages were blank.

Riya looked up. The old man was watching her with a quiet smile.

"Yours now," he said. "Write well."
