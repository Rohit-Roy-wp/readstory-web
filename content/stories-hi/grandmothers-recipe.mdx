Kitchen me elaichi aur ghee ki khushbu thi. Aarti stove ke paas khadi thi, haath me likhi hui recipe card ko dekhte hue. Uske dadi ki handwriting — chhoti, tirchi, aur margins me chhote chhote notes se bhari hui.

> *"Hamesha garam doodh use karo, thanda kabhi nahi."*
> *"Hilate waqt gaana gao — pyaar khaane me chala jaata hai."*
> *"Jaldi mat karo. Sabse achhi dishes waqt leti hain."*

Aarti ne yeh dish pehle kabhi nahi banayi thi. Yeh uski dadi ki famous kheer thi — ek mithai jo dasakوں se har family gathering me banti aayi thi.

Usne har step dhyan se follow kiya. Garam doodh. Dheere se gaana. Jaldi mat karo.

Jab woh tayyar hui, usne pehla chammach liya. Aur phoot ke rone lagi.

Bilkul waisi hi thi jaise uski dadi banati thi.

Tab usne samjha — recipes sirf instructions nahi hoti. Woh pyaar ke khat hote hain. Ek generation se doosri tak, chammach dar chammach, pass hote aate hain.

Us raat, usne margins me apne notes likhe.

Apni beti ke liye, ek din.
