The rain came suddenly — one moment the sky was clear, the next it was pouring. Ananya stood under the bus stop, hands empty. No umbrella, no phone signal, no hope of reaching home dry.

A man appeared beside her. Quiet, unassuming, holding a large black umbrella.

"Share?" he asked.

She hesitated. Then nodded.

They walked in silence for a while. The rain drummed on the umbrella, creating a small world of its own. She noticed he walked slowly, so she could keep up.

At her gate, she turned to thank him. He simply smiled, folded the umbrella, and walked away into the rain — letting himself get drenched.

She never saw him again. But every time it rains, she remembers the man who gave her his umbrella and took the storm for himself.
