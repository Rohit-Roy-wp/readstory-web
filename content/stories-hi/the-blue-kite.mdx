Har Sunday, main aur mere papa chhat par patang udate the. Unki favourite ek neeli patang thi — haath se banayi hui, lambi laal poonchh ke saath. "Neela aasman ka rang hai," woh kehte the. "Aur aasman ki koi hadd nahi hoti."

Jab main baarah saal ka tha, woh chale gaye. Patang ek dibbe me band ho gayi, unki purani ghadiyon aur chashme ke saath.

Bees saal baad, storeroom saaf karte waqt, mujhe woh dibba mila. Patang abhi bhi wahi thi, phiki pad chuki thi lekin salamat. Ek josh me, main usse chhat par le gaya.

Bachpan ke baad maine kabhi patang nahi udayi thi. Mere haath dor se ulajh rahe the. Phir hawa ka ek jhonka aaya aur usse utha le gaya — aur upar, aur upar, jab tak woh neele aasman me ek chhota sa daag nahi ban gayi.

Main wahi khada raha, aankhon se aansu beh rahe the, unki hansi yaad karte hue.

"Neela aasman ka rang hai," maine dheere se kaha. "Aur aasman ki koi hadd nahi hoti."

Ek pal ke liye, woh phir se mere saath the.
