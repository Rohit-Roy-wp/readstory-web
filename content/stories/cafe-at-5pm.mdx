Every day at 5 PM, she sat at the corner table of the café, writing in a small notebook. Every day at 5 PM, he sat two tables away, pretending to read the newspaper.

They never spoke. Not for three months.

Then one rainy evening, the café was full. The only seat left was at her table. He asked, she nodded.

"What do you write?" he asked.

"Letters to people I've never met," she said. "And you?"

"I read the same newspaper every day," he admitted. "But only because you're here."

She blushed. He panicked. But then she smiled — and it was the most beautiful thing he had ever seen.

They talked until the café closed. About books, about fears, about the strange comfort of strangers.

They've been married for nine years now. And every day at 5 PM, they still meet at that corner table.
