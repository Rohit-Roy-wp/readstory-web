Hallway ki ghadi 3:15 par ruk gayi thi — theek us waqt jab mere dada ji guzre the. Meri dadi ne usse kabhi theek nahi karaya. "Kyun karun?" woh kehti thi. "Kuch pal ruke rehne ke laayak hote hain."

Tab mujhe samajh nahi aaya. Main aath saal ka tha.

Bees saal baad, main us ghar laut aaya. Dadi bhi chali gayi thi. Ghar khaali tha, chupchap, bikne ka intezaar karte hue.

Ghadi abhi bhi 3:15 par thi.

Main seedhiyon par baith gaya aur yaad karne laga — uske khaane ki khushbu, uski hansi, bagiche me purane gaane gungunane ka tareeka. Jab main andar aata, toh woh jo bhi kar rahi hoti, sab chhod kar bas mujhe gale lagane ka tareeka.

Maine dobara ghadi ko dekha. Aur pehli baar, mujhe samajh aaya.

Har tooti hui cheez tooti hui nahi hoti. Kuch bas ruki hui hoti hain. Intezaar karti hui.

Maine ghar nahi becha. Main wahi shift ho gaya.

Aur har din 3:15 par, main jo bhi kar raha hota hoon, sab chhod deta hoon.

Bas yaad karne ke liye.
