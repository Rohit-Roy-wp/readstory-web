Har din shaam 5 baje, woh café ke corner table par baithti thi, ek chhoti si notebook me kuch likhti thi. Har din shaam 5 baje, woh do table door baithta tha, akhbaar padhne ka natak karta tha.

Unhone kabhi baat nahi ki. Teen mahine tak nahi ki.

Phir ek baarish wali shaam, café bhara hua tha. Sirf ek hi seat bachi thi — uske table par. Usne poocha, usne sar hilaya.

"Tum kya likhti ho?" usne poocha.

"Un logon ko chitthi likhti hoon jinse kabhi mili nahi," usne kaha. "Aur tum?"

"Main roz wahi akhbaar padhta hoon," usne maan liya. "Lekin sirf isliye ki tum yahan hoti ho."

Woh sharm gayi. Woh ghabra gaya. Lekin phir woh muskurayi — aur woh sabse khoobsurat cheez thi jo usne kabhi dekhi thi.

Woh baat karte rahe jab tak café band nahi ho gaya. Kitaabon ke baare me, darr ke baare me, ajnabiyon ke us ajeeb se sukoon ke baare me.

Unki shaadi ko ab nau saal ho chuke hain. Aur har din shaam 5 baje, woh aaj bhi us corner table par milte hain.
