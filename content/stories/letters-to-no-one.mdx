I've been writing letters to you for seven years.

Not emails. Not texts. Letters. On paper, with ink, folded carefully into envelopes I never mail.

The first one was written the day you left. I was angry, hurt, confused. I wrote pages and pages, then sealed it and put it in a box.

The second one was a week later. Softer this time. I asked how you were. I told you about the weather.

The third was a year later. I forgave you. In writing.

Over the years, the letters changed. Some were long, some were short. Some were angry, some were tender. But all of them were honest.

Today, I found the box. There were 312 letters in it.

I opened the first one and read it. Then the last one. And I realised something:

I wasn't writing to you. I was writing to myself. Teaching myself to forgive, to heal, to let go.

I didn't send the letters. I burned them.

And for the first time in seven years, I slept peacefully.
