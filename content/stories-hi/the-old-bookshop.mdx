Dukaan wahan tab se thi jab se kisi ko yaad hai. Na koi sign, na naam — bas ek lakdi ka darwaza aur dararon se aati purane kaghaz ki khushbu.

Riya ne usse ittefaqan ek Sunday dopahar me dhundha, baarish se bachte hue. Andar, shelves chhat tak pahunchti thi. Hazaaron kitaabein, sab dhool se dhaki hui.

Dukaan malik, silver baalon wala ek buzurg aadmi, apni padhai se nazar nahi utha raha tha. "Aaram se dekho," usne kaha. "Kitaabein apne padhne wale chun leti hain."

Woh ek ghanta bhatakti rahi. Phir ek chhoti leather-bound kitaab uski nazar me aayi — na title, na author, bas cover par ek phiki hui gulab ki sketch thi.

Jab usne usse khola, pehle page par likha tha:

> *"Agar tumhe yeh kitaab mili hai, iska matlab tum hi iske laayak the. Apni kahani yahan likho. Aage badhao."*

Baki saare page khaali the.

Riya ne upar dekha. Buzurg aadmi usse ek shaant muskaan ke saath dekh raha tha.

"Ab tumhari hai," usne kaha. "Achha likho."
