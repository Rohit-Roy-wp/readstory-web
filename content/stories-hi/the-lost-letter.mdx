Woh ek baarish wala Mangalwar tha jab Maya ko ek purani library ki kitaab ke panno ke beech ek khat mila. Lifafa umar ke saath peela pad chuka tha, us kisi ko address kiya gaya tha jo ab us pate par nahi rehta tha.

Usne usse dhyan se khola. Kaghaz uski ungliyon ke neeche chatakh raha tha, jaise dasakوں se chhua hi na gaya ho.

> "Agar tum yeh padh rahe ho, toh mujhe umeed hai tum theek ho. Main kabhi alvida keh nahi paayi..."

Yeh shabd usse rok gaye. Usne aage padha, aur khat khatam hone tak, usse pata chal gaya tha ki jisse yeh likha gaya tha, usse dhundhna hi padega. Ek naam, ek gali, ek tareekh — teen chhoti chhoti details jo usse poore shehar me le jaayengi.

Us raat, usne apni talash shuru ki.

Usse nahi pata tha ki usse kya milega, ya jisse woh dhundh rahi hai woh zinda bhi hai ya nahi. Lekin kuch khat, usne samjha, gum rehne ke liye nahi hote.
