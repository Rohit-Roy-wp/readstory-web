It was a rainy Tuesday when Maya found the letter tucked between the pages of an old library book. The envelope was yellowed with age, addressed to someone who no longer lived at that address.

She opened it carefully. The paper crackled under her fingers, as if it hadn't been touched in decades.

> "If you're reading this, I hope you're doing well. I never got to say goodbye..."

The words stopped her. She read on, and by the end of the letter, she knew she had to find the person it was meant for. A name, a street, a date — three tiny details that would send her across the city.

That night, she began her search.

She didn't know what she would find, or whether the person she was looking for was even still alive. But some letters, she realized, are not meant to stay lost.
