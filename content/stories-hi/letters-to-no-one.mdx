# Kisi Ko Na Likhay Khat

Kabhi kabhi hum bahut kuch likhna chahte hain, lekin likh nahi paate. Ya likh toh lete hain, lekin bhej nahi paate.

Yeh kahani ek aise hi khat ki hai...

**Pehla Khat**

Priya,

Tumhe yaad hai woh pehli baarish? Jab hum dono chhat pe khade the aur tumne kaha tha — "Yeh baarish kabhi rukegi nahi."

Main aaj bhi wahi sochta hoon. Baarish ruk gayi, lekin teri yaadein nahi rukti.

Tera,
Rohit
