Baarish achanak aa gayi — ek pal pehle aasman saaf tha, agle hi pal barsaat ho rahi thi. Ananya bus stop ke neeche khadi thi, haath khaali. Na chhatri, na phone ka signal, na umeed ghar sookhi pahunchne ki.

Ek aadmi uske paas aaya. Shaant, sadharan, haath me ek badi kaali chhatri.

"Share karein?" usne poocha.

Woh hichkichayi. Phir sar hilaya.

Woh kuch der chupchaap chalte rahe. Baarish chhatri par thap thap ho rahi thi, jaise apni hi ek chhoti si duniya ban gayi ho. Usne dekha ki woh dheere chalta raha, taaki woh saath chal sake.

Uske gate par, woh mukhade kar shukriya kehne ke liye. Usne bas muskuraya, chhatri band ki, aur baarish me chala gaya — khud bheegne dete hue.

Woh usse dobara kabhi nahi mili. Lekin har baar jab baarish hoti hai, usse woh aadmi yaad aata hai jisne usse apni chhatri di aur toofan khud apne naam kar liya.
